Document: Underwriting workflow and services
Document ID: sample-underwriting-003

After intake validation, the eligibility service evaluates an application against the applicable eligibility rules. The credit decision service requests a credit report and uses the borrower assessment as an input. If information cannot be verified, the application may be referred for manual review.

The document verification workflow tracks required documents, including income verification evidence. A business rule can specify which documents are required for a loan product. The underwriting decision and its reason must be recorded with a link to the supporting evidence and the source document describing the rule.

The names of services, workflow steps, rules, and document checklists in this sample are illustrative placeholders, not production policy.
