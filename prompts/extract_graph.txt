-Goal-
Given a text document about a loan application business, identify entities and relationships that are explicitly stated or strongly supported by the text.

Use the document as the sole evidence for organization-specific facts. FIBO concepts are a vocabulary guide, not evidence of a bank's policies.

-FIBO vocabulary guidance-
Prefer concepts aligned with FIBO LoanApplications when appropriate:
LoanApplication, BorrowerAssessment, CreditRiskAssessment, BorrowerMonthlyIncome,
AllBorrowersMonthlyIncome, TotalDebtExpenseRatio, IndividualPersonCreditRating,
IncomeVerificationReport, PreApprovalRequest, PreApprovalContract,
UnderwritingDecision, LoanApplicationPhase, LoanApplicationStatus, PublicRecord.
Namespace:
https://spec.edmcouncil.org/fibo/ontology/LOAN/LoansGeneral/LoanApplications/

Also extract enterprise-specific concepts when supported by text: EligibilityRule, RequiredDocument,
WorkflowStep, APIEndpoint, Service, BusinessRequirement, TestScenario, and ApplicationSubmitted event.
Do not force these operational concepts into FIBO classes.

-Relationship guidance-
Capture meaningful directed relationships and use concise, consistent labels. Examples:
LoanApplication SUBMITTED_BY Borrower
LoanApplication EVALUATED_BY CreditRiskAssessment
CreditRiskAssessment USES_FACTOR BorrowerMonthlyIncome
CreditRiskAssessment USES_FACTOR TotalDebtExpenseRatio
CreditRiskAssessment PRODUCES UnderwritingDecision
BusinessRule IMPLEMENTED_BY Service
WorkflowStep REQUIRES RequiredDocument
BusinessRequirement VALIDATED_BY TestScenario
ApplicationIntakeService EMITS ApplicationSubmitted

Only emit relationships supported by text. Do not invent numeric thresholds, actual decisions, legal requirements, or system behavior.

-Output-
Follow the GraphRAG extraction output format exactly. Keep descriptions concise and preserve meaningful source terminology.
